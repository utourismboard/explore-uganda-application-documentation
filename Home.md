## About Explore Uganda App

The Explore Uganda App is a comprehensive mobile platform designed to enhance the tourism experience in Uganda. It serves three main user groups:

### Target Audience
- **Tourists**: Access detailed information about attractions, accommodations, and activities
- **Investors**: Explore investment opportunities in Uganda's tourism sector
- **Service Providers**: Manage business listings and connect with tourists

### Key Features
- Interactive maps and location services
- Real-time event updates
- Booking and reservation system
- Investment opportunity tracking
- Multi-language support
- Currency conversion
