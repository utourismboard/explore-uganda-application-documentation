## Getting Started

### Installation
1. **iOS Installation**
   - Open App Store
   - Search for "Explore Uganda"
   - Tap "Get" to download
   - Open the app

2. **Android Installation**
   - Open Google Play Store
   - Search for "Explore Uganda"
   - Tap "Install"
   - Open the app

### System Requirements
| Platform | Requirements |
|----------|--------------|
| iOS | iPhone 6s or later, iOS 13.0+ |
| Android | 2GB RAM, Android 8.0+ |
| Network | 3G or better |

### First-Time Setup
```mermaid
graph TD
    A[Open App] --> B[Welcome Screen]
    B --> C[Choose Sign-up Method]
    C --> D[Complete Profile]
    D --> E[Onboarding Tour]
    E --> F[Main Dashboard]
```
